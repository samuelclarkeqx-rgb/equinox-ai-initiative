01 · Daily Operations & Workforce Management

Daily Operations

Human — Abhinav
Attendance & HC Management
Monitor daily attendance and ensure required headcount is maintained to meet support coverage expectations.
AI Partner
Auto Attendance Dashboard
Automatically pulls attendance data, flags absences, calculates real-time headcount vs required coverage, alerts Abhinav when HC falls below threshold.
Handoff: Abhinav reviews alerts and takes action on coverage gaps.
Human — Abhinav
Shrinkage Management
Track and update shrinkage for absences and adjust coverage plans accordingly.
AI Partner
Auto Shrinkage Tracker
Automatically calculates shrinkage percentages, updates records in real time, and generates daily shrinkage reports with trend analysis.
Handoff: Abhinav reviews report and adjusts coverage plan.
Human — Abhinav
Ticket Quality Review
Review agent responses before submission to ensure accuracy, quality, and appropriate customer communication.
AI Partner
AI Pre-Submission QA Check
Automatically reviews every agent response before submission — checks tone, accuracy, completeness, flags issues with suggested corrections. Surfaces only flagged tickets to Abhinav.
Handoff: Abhinav reviews AI-flagged tickets only — clean tickets pass automatically.
Human — Abhinav
Support Queue Management
Actively handle support tickets when required to maintain queue health.
AI Partner
Queue Health Monitor
Continuously monitors queue volume, aging tickets, and SLA risk. Alerts Abhinav when queue health drops and identifies which tickets need immediate attention.
Handoff: Abhinav decides whether to handle personally or reassign.
02 · Technical Support & Knowledge Management

Knowledge Management

Human — Abhinav
KCS Article Creation
Convert weekly session recordings and ticket resolutions into help articles and maintain them in SharePoint.
AI Partner
Auto KCS Article Generator
Automatically transcribes session recordings, extracts key resolutions, and generates a structured KCS article draft ready for Abhinav to review and publish.
Handoff: Abhinav reviews draft, makes edits, approves for SharePoint.
Human — Abhinav
Ticket Troubleshooting & Coaching
Collaborate with agents on complex tickets and document troubleshooting discussions.
AI Partner
AI Troubleshooting Suggester
For complex tickets, AI searches historical cases and knowledge base to suggest relevant troubleshooting steps — giving Abhinav a starting point before the coaching session begins.
Handoff: Abhinav reviews suggestions, adds expertise, coaches agent.
Human — Abhinav
Weekly Knowledge Sessions
Conduct weekly team syncs to strengthen product knowledge and address recurring challenges.
AI Partner
Session Prep & Summary Agent
Before each session: AI identifies top recurring issues from the week's tickets to inform the agenda. After each session: AI auto-generates a summary and distributes it to the team.
Handoff: Abhinav conducts the session — AI handles prep and post-session documentation.
03 · Performance & Business Reporting

Reporting & KPIs

Human — Abhinav
KPI & Performance Tracking
Monitor team KPIs, operational metrics, and individual performance against defined expectations.
AI Partner
Auto KPI Dashboard
Automatically aggregates ticket data, calculates all KPIs in real time, tracks individual agent performance trends, and flags anyone falling below expectations.
Handoff: Abhinav reviews dashboard, interprets trends, takes action.
Human — Abhinav
QBR Insights & Leadership Updates
Prepare data points, performance insights, and operational trends for quarterly business reviews and leadership presentations.
AI Partner
Auto Report Generator
Automatically compiles performance data, calculates trends, identifies highlights and areas of concern, and generates a structured QBR report draft ready for Abhinav to present.
Handoff: Abhinav adds context, narrative, and delivers to leadership.
