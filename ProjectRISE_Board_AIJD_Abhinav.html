Human Role
Who Abhinav is and what he is responsible for
▾
Role Title
Equinox Team Lead — Board Process
Role Purpose
Abhinav exists to ensure the Board L1 support team delivers accurate, timely, and high-quality responses to Board customers — while building the team's knowledge, capability, and consistency over time.
Primary Outcomes
Abhinav is ultimately responsible for:
  • Team FRT consistently under 20 minutes across all Board LOBs
  • Team QA score at 95%+ (Cloud & Product) and 100% (Premium)
  • Service level maintained at 95%+
  • Team shrinkage kept under 10%
  • Agents improving in quality, knowledge, and speed over time
Key Responsibilities
  • Attendance & HC management — 15 min/day
  • Shrinkage management — 15 min/day
  • Support queue management — 1.5 hrs/day
  • Ticket quality review — 1 hr/day
  • Real-time QA monitoring — 1 hr/day
  • Ticket troubleshooting & agent coaching — 2.5 hrs/day
  • Resolution & knowledge capture — 2 hrs/week
  • KCS article creation — 45 min/article
  • Weekly knowledge sessions — 1 hr/session
  • KPI & performance tracking — 2 hrs/week
  • QBR insights & reporting — 6 hrs/QBR
  • Leadership performance updates — 1.5 hrs/week
What Only Abhinav Can Do
  • Candidate interviews & assessment
  • Stakeholder & partner management
  • Team huddles, motivation & culture
  • L2 hiring coordination
2
AI Role
Board Team Lead Copilot — one AI partner, multiple capabilities
▾
AI Role Name
Board Team Lead Copilot
Purpose
Help Abhinav run a higher-performing team with less manual effort — by handling data collection, monitoring, drafting, and flagging — so Abhinav can focus on coaching, judgment, and leadership.
Boundaries
AI suggests, flags, and drafts only. Abhinav reviews and decides. AI never communicates with agents, clients, or leadership directly. AI never makes performance decisions about people.
Capabilities

Attendance & HC Monitor

Auto-pulls daily attendance, flags HC shortfalls, alerts Abhinav in real time.

Shrinkage Tracker

Auto-calculates shrinkage daily, generates trend report.

Queue Health Monitor

Monitors queue volume and SLA risk, alerts Abhinav when action needed.

Pre-Submission QA Check

Screens all agent responses before submission, surfaces only flagged tickets to Abhinav.

KCS Article Generator

Auto-drafts KCS articles from resolved tickets and session recordings.

Troubleshooting Suggester

Suggests relevant steps from historical cases before coaching sessions begin.

Session Prep & Summary

Prepares weekly session agenda from ticket trends, auto-generates post-session summary.

KPI Dashboard & Reports

Auto-aggregates all KPIs in real time, drafts QBR and leadership update reports.

Team Leader AI
How the AI gets managed and continuously improved
▾
What the Team Leader AI Monitors
  • Abhinav's performance against human KRAs
  • AI Copilot's leading indicators — flag accuracy, draft quality, alert relevance
  • Combined Human + AI outcomes vs baseline
What it Identifies
  • Coaching opportunities for Abhinav — patterns in agent errors he hasn't addressed
  • Recurring AI failures — capabilities not working or not being used
  • Knowledge gaps in the team — recurring ticket types with wrong resolutions
  • AI prompt or instruction improvements needed
