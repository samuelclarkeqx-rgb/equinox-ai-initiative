01 · Objective

Why Project RISE?

The future of work at Equinox is not human vs AI — it is human with AI. Project RISE is our initiative to build a network of autonomous AI agents, each with a defined role, working alongside every Equinox team member to observe, decide, and act — under human guidance and oversight.


This is not about replacing people. It is about giving every person at Equinox an AI partner that handles their repetitive, time-consuming tasks — freeing them to focus on judgment, communication, and relationship management.

AGENTIC AI · Every Human + Every AI Agent = 10x Output
02 · AI Job Description

What is an AIJD?

Just as every human has a Job Description — every AI agent at Equinox will have an AI Job Description (AIJD) that defines exactly what it does, how it performs, and when the human takes over.

AIJD Element Description
RoleWhat process or function does this AI agent support?
ResponsibilitiesWhat specific tasks does it perform daily?
KRAsHow do we measure its performance? (e.g. time saved, accuracy, tickets resolved)
Tools UsedWhich platforms, APIs, or data sources does it connect to?
Agentic CapabilityDoes it respond only — or does it monitor, flag, and execute actions autonomously?
Human HandoffWhen does the AI stop and the human take over?

04 · Deployment Strategy

How We Deploy Our Agent Network

This framework cannot be built from a desk. It requires direct observation and conversation with every process owner and their agents before any AI solution is designed.

1

Google Form Survey — All Process Owners + Agents

A structured survey rolled out to every team member — giving ground-level agents a safe, low-pressure way to share honest feedback about their daily work.

  • Which tasks consume the most time in a day?
  • Which tasks are repeated in exactly the same way every day?
  • Where do they manually search for information — SOPs, docs, old tickets?
  • If one task could be automated, what would it be?
  • What part of the process is confusing or frustrating?
2

Response Analysis & Prioritization

Form responses are analyzed to identify where AI will deliver the fastest, most visible impact.

PriorityCriteriaAction
High PriorityHigh volume + highly repetitiveApproach first for deep dive
Quick WinSimple task, easy to automateBuild early to show results
ComplexMulti-step or unclear processApproach after simpler wins
3

Targeted 1-on-1 Sessions

Based on prioritized responses, targeted sessions are scheduled — starting with high-priority process owners and their agents. These sessions observe actual tools, steps, and workflows to confirm the AI opportunity.

4

Draft AIJDs

For each process, write an AI Job Description defining what the agent will do, how it will be measured, and when the human takes over.

  • Pilot with 2–3 process owners first — gather feedback before full rollout
  • Set up GitHub repository for all Equinox AI tools and version control
  • Establish AI usage guidelines and data security SOPs
  • Present pilot results at Equinox Town Hall — December 2026
