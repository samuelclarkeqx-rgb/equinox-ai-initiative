03 · Proposal — Suchirmay

AI Case Assistance Agent

🤖 Intelligent Case Assistance for L1 Support

Currently agents manually copy case descriptions into ChatGPT, generate summaries, then separately search Salesforce for historical cases. This is a multi-step manual process for every single ticket.

Proposed Solution: An AI Case Assistance Agent integrated with Salesforce that automatically:

  • Analyzes the customer description on ticket creation
  • Generates an accurate internal case summary
  • Prepares a professional acknowledgement for the first response
  • Searches historical cases for similar issues, resolutions, and troubleshooting approaches
  • Presents all recommendations to the agent before they respond

Example: If a customer reports a FlexGrid issue, the AI identifies similar historical cases, recommends the most relevant troubleshooting steps, and suggests diagnostic questions — all before the agent types a single word.

High Priority

L1 Agent Level — Repetitive Activities

Jamal documented 6 specific repetitive activities performed daily by L1 Support agents — these represent the ground-level friction that slows down every ticket.

7
Case Summary Preparation
Source: Jamal

Pain Area

Every new ticket requires manually reading, understanding, and summarizing before it can be forwarded

AI Opportunity

Auto-generate concise internal case summary on ticket creation — product, environment, version, error identified automatically

Expected Benefit

Significant time saved per ticket — especially for complex tickets with logs and screenshots

Quick Win
8
Duplicate & Spam Notifications
Source: Jamal

Pain Area

Hundreds of monitoring alerts, OOO replies, thank-you emails, spam — each requiring manual open, review, and close. Mass events can exceed 500 notifications

AI Opportunity

Auto-detect and close duplicate alerts, OOO replies, thank-you messages, spam, and mass notification events without agent involvement

Expected Benefit

Major time saving — agents focus only on genuine customer issues

Quick Win
9
Portal Registration Process
Source: Jamal

Pain Area

Every registration request requires 7 manual Salesforce verification steps before access can be granted

AI Opportunity

Auto-verify account in Salesforce — SSO status, account type, eligibility, access level — present result to agent for final approval

Expected Benefit

Faster registration processing, less repetitive Salesforce lookups

High Priority
10
Knowledge Base Research
Source: Jamal

Pain Area

Agents manually search 6+ sources — Salesforce, Help.Board.com, Community Portal, internal docs, Release Notes, Known Issues

AI Opportunity

Unified AI search across all sources — returns ranked relevant results for the specific issue in seconds

Expected Benefit

Drastically reduced research time, consistent answers across agents

High Priority
11
Standard Email Responses
Source: Jamal

Pain Area

Acknowledgements, screenshot requests, log requests, closure emails — templates exist but agents still manually select, edit, and customize each one

AI Opportunity

AI suggests the right template automatically based on ticket context — pre-filled and ready to send

Expected Benefit

Faster responses, consistent tone and format across all agents

Source: Jamal

Pain Area
