03 · Brian's AI Proposal

SFDC Smart Alert System

Brian identified a specific, high-value AI opportunity from his daily experience — this is his own proposed solution.

🔔 SFDC Customer Update Alert

The Problem: Board International does not update Salesforce frequently. Important customer info — like special SLAs, triage rules, and account-specific notes — is shared via Microsoft Teams and tracked manually in a Google Sheet. Agents must remember to check this sheet before handling every ticket.

Brian's Proposed Solution:

  • Agent opens a case on Salesforce
  • AI automatically checks the Google Sheet for any relevant update matching the customer, account, or issue type
  • If a match is found — a pop-up alert appears on SFDC immediately
  • Agent reads the alert and proceeds with the correct action

Example 1: UNHCR case opens — AI alerts "UNHCR is not a Premium customer despite SFDC showing otherwise. Follow non-Premium SOP."

Example 2: Primark case with product issue — AI alerts "All Primark product-related cases must be triaged to the Wipro queue."

⚡ Impact: Eliminates manual Google Sheet checking, prevents SOP errors, ensures correct routing on every ticket.
