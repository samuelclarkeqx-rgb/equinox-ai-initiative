Human Role
Who Vaibhav is and what he is responsible for
▾
Role Title
L1 Support Agent — Board Process (Most Tenured)
Role Purpose
Vaibhav exists to deliver accurate, timely, and high-quality first-line support to Board customers — while serving as a knowledge anchor for the team through his tenure and product expertise.
Primary Outcomes
  • First response to customers under 20 minutes across all Board LOBs
  • QA score at 95%+ (Cloud & Product) and 100% (Premium)
  • Correct triage — Premium and critical cases identified and routed accurately
  • Knowledge articles published consistently from resolved tickets
  • New agents onboarded and ramped to team standards
Key Responsibilities — Time-Measurable
  • Ticket quality review — 10 min/ticket, 8 hrs/day
  • Ticket troubleshooting & research — 10 min/ticket, 5 hrs/day
  • SFDC customer alert check (Google Sheet) — 3 min/ticket, 45 min/day
  • Community queue management — 45 min/ticket, low frequency
  • Knowledge article creation — 1.5 hrs/article
  • Training & onboarding new agents — 23 hrs/week
Key Responsibilities — Always-On
These cannot be measured in time — they require constant attention every minute of every shift:

Queue management & monitoring — Vaibhav must manually check a Google Sheet per ticket to identify Premium customers before triaging to Board T2.

Infrastructure monitoring (PagerDuty) — Critical alerts currently reach only one agent. Rest of team is unaware.

Agent support & guidance — Always available reactively throughout the day.
What Only Vaibhav Can Do
  • Customer & client calls — relationship and complex investigation
  • Training & onboarding new agents — judgment, empathy, culture
  • Stakeholder & Academy collaboration
2
AI Role
Board L1 Support Copilot — one AI partner, multiple capabilities
▾
AI Role Name
Board L1 Support Copilot
Purpose
Help Vaibhav deliver faster, more accurate and consistent support by providing customer context, research assistance, quality checks, queue monitoring, and knowledge creation — so Vaibhav can focus on solving complex problems and coaching others.
Boundaries
AI suggests, flags, and drafts only. Vaibhav reviews and decides before any response is sent. AI never communicates with customers directly. AI never makes triage or escalation decisions — it flags and Vaibhav acts.
Capabilities

SFDC Smart Alert — Vaibhav's Proposal

On ticket open, AI auto-checks Google Sheet for Premium status or special SLA — pops up alert on SFDC instantly. No manual lookup needed.

Troubleshooting Suggester

Instantly searches Salesforce, Help.Board.com, Community Portal, internal docs and resolved tickets — surfaces relevant steps before Vaibhav opens the case.

Pre-Send Quality Check

Before response is sent, AI checks tone, accuracy, completeness and SOP adherence — flags issues with suggestions.

Queue & Priority Monitor

Monitors queue continuously — alerts Vaibhav when SLA is at risk or a high-priority customer submits.

Knowledge Article Generator

On ticket close, AI auto-drafts a knowledge article from the conversation — Vaibhav reviews and publishes.

Team-Wide PagerDuty Alerts

AI classifies PagerDuty severity — sends Slack notification to entire team for critical alerts, not just one agent.

Community Queue Monitor

Monitors Community queue — flags issues and suggests responses based on similar resolved posts.

Case Summary Generator

Auto-generates internal case summary on ticket creation — product, environment, error identified automatically.
