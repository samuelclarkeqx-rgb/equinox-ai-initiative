Human Role
Who Robert is and what he is responsible for
▾
Role Title
Quality Analyst — Board & KIPU Processes
Role Purpose
Robert exists to drive service excellence across Board and KIPU processes — ensuring agents deliver consistently high-quality interactions, identifying performance risks early, and creating a feedback loop that continuously improves both agent behaviour and process standards.
Primary Outcomes
  • QA scores maintained at target — 95%+ Cloud & Product, 100% Premium
  • Agents receiving structured, timely feedback that improves their performance
  • Quality risks identified and escalated before they impact SLA or CSAT
  • Consistent evaluation standards across all auditors and clients
  • Leadership and clients receiving accurate quality reporting on time
Key Responsibilities
  • Quality audits across Chat, Voice, and Email — Board & KIPU
  • DSAT & NPS analysis — identify trends and customer pain points
  • Weekly QA feedback and coaching recommendations to agents
  • Weekly & monthly performance reports — quality metrics and insights
  • Monthly RED Alert Reports — critical quality and operational risks
  • QA calibration sessions — internal teams and clients
  • JMS sessions with Team Leaders — performance review and alignment
  • Root Cause Analysis (RCA) for SLA breach tickets
  • Process gap identification and corrective action recommendations
  • Continuous improvement monitoring — adherence to standards and SOPs

2
AI Role
Board QA Copilot — one AI partner, multiple capabilities
▾
AI Role Name
Board QA Copilot
Purpose
Help Robert achieve broader, faster, and more consistent quality coverage — by automating ticket pre-screening, surfacing patterns in agent errors, and drafting reports and feedback — so Robert can focus on coaching, calibration, and quality governance.
Boundaries
AI pre-screens and flags only. Robert reviews every AI assessment before feedback is shared with agents or leadership. AI never delivers feedback to agents directly. AI never finalises RCA or calibration outcomes — those require Robert's human judgment.
Capabilities

Automated QA Pre-Screening

AI evaluates tickets against the scorecard criteria — flags likely fails and near-misses for Robert to review. Clean tickets surface separately.

DSAT & NPS Pattern Analyser

Automatically analyses DSAT and NPS data — identifies recurring themes, agent patterns, and customer pain points before Robert reviews.

Feedback Draft Generator

Drafts structured weekly QA feedback for each agent based on audit findings — Robert reviews, edits, and delivers.

Report Generator

Auto-compiles weekly and monthly quality reports — metrics, trends, and highlights — ready for Robert to review and distribute.

RED Alert Detector

Monitors quality metrics continuously — flags when scores or trends cross RED Alert thresholds for Robert to escalate.

RCA Assistant

For SLA breach tickets, AI surfaces ticket history, agent actions, and similar past breaches — gives Robert a starting point for RCA investigation.

Calibration Prep

Selects representative audit samples for calibration sessions — covers edge cases, disputed tickets, and common error patterns.

Process Gap Identifier

Tracks recurring SOP deviations across agents and ticket types — surfaces patterns that indicate process gaps vs individual agent errors.

AI Quality Partner
How Robert's AI partner gets managed and continuously improved
▾
What the AI Quality Partner Monitors
  • Robert's QA audit coverage and feedback delivery consistency
  • AI Copilot's leading indicators — pre-screen accuracy, draft quality, alert detection
  • Combined Human + AI quality outcomes vs baseline
What it Identifies
  • Scorecard criteria where AI pre-screening is consistently wrong
  • Agents with recurring errors that coaching hasn't resolved
  • Process gaps vs individual agent errors — pattern distinction
  • AI capabilities not being used by Robert — flagging underutilisation
  • Calibration misalignments — where AI and Robert consistently disagree
